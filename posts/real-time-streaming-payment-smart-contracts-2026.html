Real-Time Streaming Payments on Soroban: Linear Vesting Math & Precision

Formal verification of continuous token vesting algorithms, pro-rata cancellation splits, and integer rounding invariant safety.

Financial Invariant: In continuous token streaming protocols, calculating accrued balances via accrued = (total * elapsed) / duration must preserve strict zero-sum conservation: vested + unvested == totalDeposit at all times, without rounding errors leaking protocol liquidity.
