Production RAG Retrieval: BM25 Relevance Floors & Vocabulary Pruning

Eliminating false-positive document retrieval in high-volume enterprise knowledge bases without adding neural reranker latency.

Retrieval Principle: Pure vector cosine similarity often returns irrelevant "hallucinated near-neighbors" when queried with rare domain tokens or error codes. Applying a hybrid BM25 sparse index with an IDF-weighted relevance floor rejects 96% of out-of-domain queries before LLM context generation, preventing silent context pollution.

Production Hardening Guideline

Calibrate your BM25 relevance threshold dynamically based on the sum of IDF weights for non-stopword query tokens. When all candidate documents score below the IDF floor, immediately return a structured no_match intake payload rather than injecting low-confidence noise into agent contexts.
