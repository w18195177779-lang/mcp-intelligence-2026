Cross-Chain Escrow & Atomic Settlement: HTLC vs Relayed State Machines

Evaluating atomic swap finality, griefing vectors, and relayer security across heterogeneous blockchain corridors.

Comparative Synthesis: While traditional Hash Time-Locked Contracts (HTLCs) offer cryptographic atomicity without trusted third parties, they expose market makers to free American call options during lengthy timeout windows. Modern relayed state machine bridges with optimistic challenge windows cut finality latency from hours to under 30 seconds.
