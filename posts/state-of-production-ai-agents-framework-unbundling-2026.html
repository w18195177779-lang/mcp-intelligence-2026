State of Production AI Agents in 2026: The Great Framework Unbundling

Empirical post-mortem on why 82% of enterprise AI teams replaced heavy orchestration frameworks with deterministic finite state machines and SQLite event buses.

Key Architectural Thesis: In 2024–2025, agent development was dominated by bloated abstraction layers that leaked memory, hid LLM context prompts, and failed under long-horizon retries. In 2026, enterprise production systems have shifted to lightweight, event-driven state machines with deterministic tool schemas and SQLite persistence.
